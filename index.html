Questions we hear often

Waterproofing stops water from reaching the building's structure. It's needed on roofs, basements, tanks, pools, bathrooms, and any area exposed to moisture or rainwater.

العزل الحراري التقليدي (ألواح جفالي أو بولي ستايرين) يُركَّب كطبقات منفصلة. عزل الفوم يُرش كطبقة متصلة بلا فواصل، فيجمع بين العزل الحراري والمائي في آن واحد، وهو مناسب خاصة للأسطح الكبيرة.

Traditional thermal insulation (rock wool or polystyrene boards) is installed as separate layers. Foam insulation is sprayed as one seamless layer, combining thermal and water insulation at once — especially suited to large roofs.

Yes — we serve every region of Saudi Arabia. Our head office is in Riyadh, and we carry out projects in other cities and regions depending on the project's scope.

Reach out via WhatsApp or the contact form at the bottom of the page with a short description of the project and its location, and we'll get in touch to arrange a site visit.

Roofs, basements, tanks, pools, bathrooms, kitchens, and parking or warehouse floors — each surface has an insulation type suited to how it's used.
